The rose cover used in the sharing, hiding and revealing examples is derived
from testimages/testorig.jpg in libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1. The screenshots crop and
resize its display in this application's real UI. No endorsement is implied.

Other OCR examples use this repository's synthetic test images. The credential
result shows the real inspector output for the public c2pa-rs CA.jpg fixture:
https://github.com/contentauth/c2pa-rs/blob/main/sdk/tests/fixtures/CA.jpg
The PDF example is a synthetic single-page document generated by the capture
script. No personal documents are used in these illustrations.
